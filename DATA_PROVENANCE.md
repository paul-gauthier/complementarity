# Data provenance and third-party notices

## Original experimental data

The detector acquisitions under `data/raw/mzi/` were produced by the project
authors. 

## Archived environmental inputs

The following files are preserved as retrieved inputs from third-party
providers so that the analysis can be replayed offline.

### AERONET

Files under `data/raw/conditions/*/aeronet/` are archived
AERONET records for the UCSB site. 

Source: https://aeronet.gsfc.nasa.gov/

### GOES-18

Files under `data/raw/conditions/*/goes/` are archived
GOES-18 ABI Level-2 aerosol optical depth, cloud optical depth, and total
precipitable water products. 

Source: https://noaa-goes18.s3.amazonaws.com/index.html

### NASA/IPAC IRSA

Files under `data/raw/conditions/*/irsa/` are archived
NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive query responses and tables. 

Source: https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/

### Aviation weather

Files under `data/raw/conditions/*/metar/` are archived
aviation-weather observations for station KSBA. 

Source: https://aviationweather.gov/
